Invisio Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Invisio is made by ENTR0ZY. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad or Android.

The short version

Invisio does not collect, store or share any of your data. There is no account and no sign-up. Everything you bring in is read on your device and stays there.

Your chats and documents

When you choose a file, share something to Invisio, or paste text, Invisio reads only that item. The conversations, documents and maps you make are kept only on your device. We can't see them.

Links you paste

Invisio only goes online when you paste a link for it to read:

  • Chat links (for example a shared conversation page) are opened from your device, straight from the site that hosts them, the same way your browser would open them. If that site needs you to sign in, you sign in on the site itself; Invisio never sees your password.
  • X (Twitter) post links are read through the public FxTwitter service (api.fxtwitter.com), which returns the public posts in that thread. FxTwitter receives the post's address, as any website receives a page request.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Saving and sharing

Maps you save go into the app's own folder on your device (on iPhone and iPad: Files → On My iPhone → Invisio). Maps you send or print go only where you send them, through your device's own share sheet or print screen.

No tracking

Invisio has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Children

Invisio does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a conversation inside the app, delete saved maps in the Files app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
