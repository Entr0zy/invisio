Invisio Support

Something not working, or a question? It helps to say which device you are on and what kind of chat or file you brought in. Email entrozy@protonmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two days.

Common questions

What can I bring in? Chat exports (including large ZIP files), an X (Twitter) data archive, PDF, Word, OpenDocument, RTF, EPUB, slides, email and plain text. You can also share a link, text or file to Invisio from another app.

Where did my saved map go? On iPhone and iPad: Files → On My iPhone (or iPad) → Invisio.

Is anything uploaded? No. Everything is read on your device. Invisio only goes online to read a link you paste.
